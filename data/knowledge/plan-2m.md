# Red Rocks NIL Hub: the $2M plan (planning estimates, not forecasts)

Goal: $2,000,000 a year in NIL dollars paid to Red Rocks athletes. All figures below are planning estimates, not forecasts.

## Three money streams
- Sponsors, $900K: 4 Anchor Partners × $150,000 + 20 Meet Night × $7,500 + 60 Athlete Spotlight × $2,500.
- Athlete campaigns, about $402K: 20 youth clinics × $5,000 + 30 athlete-pitched brand campaigns × $5,000 + 6,200 signed drops × $35 × 70% to the athlete ($151,900).
- Fans, about $701K: about 3,000 members (450 current WRTH donors + 26.1% of 9,800 season ticket holders = 3,008) × $15 a month average × 12 months ≈ $541K, plus 8,000 one-time supports (Quests, shout-outs) × $20 = $160K.
- Total: $2,003,340 a year to athletes (sponsors $900,000 + athlete campaigns $401,900 + fans $701,440).

## Sponsor packages
- Athlete Spotlight: $2,500. For a small business near campus. One AI-matched athlete, one Reel and one story set, a tracked offer code.
- Meet Night: $7,500. For a mid-size Utah brand. Four athletes promote one meet-week offer, a QR offer for every VIP Pass member, a results report.
- Anchor Partner: $150,000 per season. Whole roster shares the fee, a season-long tracked offer, monthly results reports, "presented by" credit on the meet-day Live TikTok.
- Athletes keep 100% of every package price. Sponsors pay a 15% service fee on top (about $135K a year in the plan), which runs the hub and is not counted in the $2M.
- Package prices are not athlete rates. For one athlete's own price, the business uses Request rate.

## Fan memberships
- Insider $10 a month, All-Access $25 a month, Inner Circle $50 a month. The plan assumes an average of $15 a month across tiers.
- 100% of fan contributions reach athletes, minus card processing.

## Quarterly milestones
Q1 $250K → Q2 $750K → Q3 $1.5M → Q4 $2M (cumulative).

## Fan engagement features
- Member ↔ athlete DMs are live: members only, AI drafts, athlete approves.
