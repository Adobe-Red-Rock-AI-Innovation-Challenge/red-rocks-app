# Utah Gymnastics: program facts (checked November 2026)

- 2026 season: Utah won its second straight Big 12 regular-season and conference titles. The team's streak of 49 straight national championship appearances ended. Avery Neff placed 7th all-around and 4th on floor at nationals; Neff and Ella Zirbes earned All-America honors. Avery Neff was Big 12 Gymnast of the Year and Ana Padurariu Big 12 Specialist of the Year. Source: Deseret News, May 1, 2026.
- Head coach Carly Dockendorf's contract runs through 2031. Steve Arkell, a USA National Team coach, joined as bars coach. Source: Deseret News, May 1, 2026.
- 2027 NCAA Women's Gymnastics Championship: Ariat Arena (formerly Dickies Arena), Fort Worth, Texas, April 15 and 17, 2027. Source: Ariat Arena.
- Attendance: 12,207 fans per meet in 2025 (Salt Lake Tribune, March 2025). NCAA record crowd 16,019 against Michigan in 2015 (Utah Athletics).
- Revenue sharing: Utah can share up to about $20.5M a year with athletes from July 2025; the per-sport split is not public. Athletes can still sign outside NIL deals. Source: KSL, August 2025.
- Collectives: the Crimson Collective is Utah's official NIL collective; in December 2023 it offered leased vehicles to Red Rocks gymnasts. Who Rocks the House (WRTH) is the NIL collective only for Utah Gymnastics. Source: KSL, December 2023; WRTH presentation, 2026.
